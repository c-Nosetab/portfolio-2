## Overview

A small, focused site for **MI-X** (pronounced "EM-eye-X"), a barbershop chorus in the Pioneer District. The whole job is one clear goal: help prospective singers find the chorus and take the step to join. No sprawling feature set — a clean, welcoming front door.

## Build

Built in **Angular 15** with **Angular Material**, animated with **GSAP** for smooth, personable motion, and styled in SCSS. A small build script generates the member image list at build time, so the roster stays easy to update without a heavy backend. A Sanity backend was scaffolded but ultimately not adopted — the frontend runs cleanly on its own, which keeps the site simple and cheap to host.

## What it demonstrates

Right-sizing a project: shipping a tidy, animated, purpose-built site that does one job well, without over-engineering. Also a second data point (alongside [Task Board Manager](./task-board-manager.mdx)) for comfort in Angular, and a taste of animation work via GSAP.
