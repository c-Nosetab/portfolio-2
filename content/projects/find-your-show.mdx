## Overview

**Find Your Show** is a directory platform connecting audiences, venues, and performers across the live-entertainment industry — local theater, music, comedy, and more. The bet is a **directory-first** model: aggregate and surface information and link out to existing ticketing/services rather than becoming another transactional middleman. It's my own product, in active development.

> *Find Your Show. It's closer than you think — support your local spotlight.*

## Why it's in the portfolio

This is the project that shows product thinking, not just execution. It demonstrates how I approach an app end to end: a clear positioning decision (directory-first over transactions), a distinctive design system, a properly architected full-stack codebase, and a phased roadmap toward monetization.

## Design system

The interface is deliberately **warm and theatrical** — not corporate, not generic template. It runs on a documented token system: a burgundy "curtain" primary, brass gold accents, coral "spotlight" highlights, on a parchment base, with DM Serif Display for headlines, Crimson Pro for body, and Outfit for UI. The design system lives in-repo (`system.md`, `patterns.md`, `components.md`, `implementation.md`) so the look stays consistent as the app grows.

## Architecture

A **Turborepo monorepo** with clean separation:

- `apps/web` — **Next.js 15 / React 19**, Tailwind v4, Shadcn UI.
- `apps/api` — **NestJS 11** with **Drizzle ORM** over **PostgreSQL**, documented via OpenAPI/Swagger.
- `packages/shared` — shared TypeScript types across front and back for end-to-end type safety.
- Auth via **Better-Auth**, file storage on **Cloudflare R2**.

## Engineering discipline

The repo is built like a real product, not a demo: **Vitest** unit tests and **Playwright** E2E, strict TypeScript, ESLint/Prettier, an `all-checks` gate (typecheck → test → lint → build), and a structured, phased implementation plan — core directory, then monetization (subscriptions), audience accounts, company teams/permissions, multi-venue support, and auditions.

## What it demonstrates

Product ownership from concept to code: positioning and IA decisions, a bespoke design system, a full-stack TypeScript monorepo with tested, documented APIs, and the discipline to build something maintainable and extensible over multiple phases.
