## Overview

**WagerVault** (formerly Gamble Tracker) is a data-centric mobile app for people who want granular insight into their gambling habits — tracking spend, session outcomes, and performance over time. It logs bankroll, bonuses, jackpots, and progressives; visualizes win/loss trends; computes personal *and* global Return-to-Player (RTP); and estimates year-end federal taxes from handpays. Built as a universal app, it runs on iOS, Android, and web from one codebase.

## Why it's in the portfolio

It's the mobile entry — proof I ship native cross-platform, not just web. It also shows product breadth: a real analytics engine, sensitive-data handling, and a considered brand (a split between `wagervault.app` for the product and `wagervault.casino` for community/responsible-gaming content).

## What it does

- **Session tracking** — log casino + machine, initial buy-in, additions, and cash-out; capture bonuses (Hold & Spin, Free Spins, etc.) with denom, bet, and return; record handpays with photo uploads; note progressive states (Mini → Grand).
- **RTP engine** — calculates personal and global RTP by casino, machine type, and even specific machine ID, plus bonus frequency and average bonus RTP.
- **Analytics dashboard** — charted win/loss amounts, ratios, and percentages over time (via `react-native-gifted-charts`).
- **Tax tracking** — aggregates calendar-year handpays and estimates federal tax owed, with explicit "not a tax service" disclaimers.
- **Privacy + community** — per-item Personal/Public controls (tax-form photos forced private), plus an aggregated global "last jackpot hit" feed.

## Architecture

An **Expo / React Native** app using **Expo Router** for file-based, typed navigation (grouped auth and tab routes), styled with **NativeWind**. The backend is **Supabase** — auth, Postgres (with migrations in-repo), and storage for image uploads. Animations run on **Reanimated**; the UI uses a custom type system (Space Grotesk + JetBrains Mono) for a data-forward feel.

## What it demonstrates

Cross-platform mobile development from one TypeScript codebase, a non-trivial analytics/RTP computation layer, thoughtful handling of sensitive financial data and privacy, and product/brand thinking around a regulated, responsible-gaming-conscious domain.
